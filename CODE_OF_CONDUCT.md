# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Stack Underflow is a student open source project. We want everyone to feel welcome when asking questions, contributing code, writing documentation or reviewing pull requests. We do not accept harassment or discrimination based on identity, background, experience or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient with other people.
- Give useful feedback and explain technical decisions clearly.
- Welcome beginners and help them learn.
- Respect other people's privacy and do not share private information.
- Focus discussions on the project and its educational purpose.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Insults, bullying, threats or harassment.
- Discriminatory language or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Spam, intentionally harmful content or repeated disruption.
- Sharing credentials, private tokens or other sensitive data.

## Enforcement

The maintainers, Boris Dubovoy and Adil Yergazy, may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, reject contributions or restrict participation when necessary. Decisions should be proportionate to the problem and aim to keep the community safe.

## Reporting a problem

Report concerns privately to a maintainer through an appropriate GitHub contact method. Do not publish sensitive personal information in a public issue. The maintainers will review reports as fairly and confidentially as possible and take appropriate action.

This Code of Conduct applies to the repository, its issues and pull requests, and project-related community discussions.
